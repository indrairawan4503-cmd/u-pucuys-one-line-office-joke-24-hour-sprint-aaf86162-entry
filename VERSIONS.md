# VERSIONS — Variant A: The Meeting That Should Have Been an Email

Concept: the calendar invite / meeting itself inverts — the meeting that should have been an email finally becomes one, does the whole meeting to itself, and turns the punchline on the humans. One concrete scene, one surprising turn, punchline last. Shipped version: **v8 (174 chars)**.

## v1 — 149 chars
> Our weekly sync finally got cancelled: the calendar invite attended itself, took notes, and emailed us all a summary saying it didn't need us either.

Reason: first sketch of the inversion; punchline ("didn't need us either") is soft and the setup rambles over two clauses.

## v2 — 161 chars
> The meeting that should have been an email finally became one — it replied-all to itself, scheduled a follow-up to discuss the email, and invited us as optional.

Reason: sharper premise (meeting literally becomes the email) but the follow-up-meeting gag splits the joke in two directions and the ending fizzles.

## v3 — 163 chars

Reason: "declined itself" is a nice beat, but "attended the meeting" repeats the word meeting and the timeline opener wastes budget.

## v4 — 167 chars

Reason: the email-schedules-a-meeting loop is funny, but "except the invite" lands as a shrug, not a punchline; two jokes competing.

## v5 — 146 chars

Reason: tightest skeleton so far — triple self-action ("emailed/attended/took") — but too short (146) and "marked us all optional" ends flat, no sting.
